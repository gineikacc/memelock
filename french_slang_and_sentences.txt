=================== A1: GREETINGS & INTRODUCTION ===================

1.  Hello, my name is John.
    → Bonjour, je m'appelle John.

2.  How are you?
    → Comment ça va ?

3.  I'm fine, thank you.
    → Je vais bien, merci.

4.  Good morning.
    → Bonjour.

5.  Goodbye, see you later.
    → Au revoir, à bientôt.

6.  Please and thank you.
    → S'il vous plaît et merci.

7.  Yes, please. / No, thank you.
    → Oui, s'il vous plaît. / Non, merci.

8.  I'm sorry.
    → Je suis désolé(e).

9.  Excuse me.

10. Nice to meet you.

IPA NOTES:
- French has no word-level stress; no stress marks are used in transcription.
- Nasal vowels: /ɑ̃/ (ans, en), /ɔ̃/ (on, bon), /œ̃/ (un, brun)
- The French /ʁ/ is typically a voiced uvular fricative.
- /ɲ/ represents the palatal nasal (as in "campagn" /kam.ɲ/).
- /ʃ/ = "sh" as in "she", /ʒ/ = "zh" as in "measure".

VERLAN EXPLANATION:
- Verlan is a form of French slang where syllables of a word are reversed.
  Examples: fou → ouf, fête → teuf, choper → pécho, lourd → relou,
  louche → chelou, méchant → chanmé, énervé → vénère,
  racaille → caillera.

LEVEL GUIDE:
- A1 = Beginner: basic phrases, simple sentences, everyday vocabulary.
- A2 = Elementary: routine activities, past/future events, opinions.

SAFETY NOTE:
- Slang words marked as informal/slang should NOT be used in formal
  settings (work, academic contexts, meetings).
- Some terms may be considered vulgar depending on context and region.
